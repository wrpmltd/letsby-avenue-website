The questions worth settling early.

How long does switching take, and what does it cost?

Timing and cost depend on your agreement and the information available. We'll discuss the practical position before setting a start date.

Will my tenants have to move out?

No. Changing managing agent does not itself end the tenancy. We plan around the existing tenancy and communicate the new contact and payment arrangements clearly.

Will my current agent charge me for leaving?

They may have notice-period fees, exit charges or continuing commission in their agreement. Check those terms before giving notice. If a clause or charge is disputed, get independent advice.

What happens to the tenant's deposit?

We establish how it is held and protected, then coordinate the relevant scheme process and any required permissions or updated information. It needs a proper record, not an informal transfer.

Do I have to deal with my old agent?

Once instructed and authorised, we can request the handover information from the current agent. We'll keep track of what arrives and tell you where your input is needed.
