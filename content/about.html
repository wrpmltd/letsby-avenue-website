About Letsby Avenue

Property is the business. People are the point.

We're an independent, family-run letting agency based in Leeds. We believe a good working relationship starts with listening carefully and being straightforward about what happens next.

Behind every rental is someone making decisions about a home, an investment or both. That deserves more than a standard response.

Small details have a real impact.

A repair update can help a tenant plan their day. A clear statement can answer a landlord's question before they need to ask it. A good inspection report can make the next decision easier.

That's where we think good management shows itself: in the ordinary work, handled with care and followed through.

Principles you should notice in practice.

Say what we mean

Explain the position in ordinary language. If something needs checking, say so rather than give a confident guess.

Respect both sides

A landlord needs their property looked after. A tenant needs a home that is properly managed. Good communication matters to both.

Own the next step

Be clear about who is doing what, what needs approval and what is still outstanding. Progress should be understandable.

Local enough to understand the detail.

Different streets attract different renters. Property condition, transport, nearby employers and everyday amenities all shape the conversation.

We bring that local perspective to our work across Leeds, Pudsey, Stanningley, Bramley, Farsley and the wider West Yorkshire area. If you're unsure whether we cover your property, send us the postcode.
