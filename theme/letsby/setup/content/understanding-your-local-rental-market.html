One property. Four market influences.

A realistic rental valuation looks at the whole picture. These are the factors that help turn an asking price into genuine tenant interest.

Location makes a difference

Transport links, schools, employment hubs and the character of a neighbourhood all shape what tenants are prepared to pay. We look beyond the postcode to the details that matter.

Follow real tenant demand

A family home and a city apartment attract different renters. Understanding who is looking, and what they need, helps position your property for the right audience.

Presentation adds appeal

Condition, layout, natural light and running costs influence every viewing. Thoughtful improvements and clear photographs help tenants see the value in your property.

Know your competition

Compare homes with similar space, condition and features. Asking prices are only part of the picture: time on the market and achieved rents matter too.

02

The highest asking rent isn't always the best return.

An ambitious price can look attractive on paper. But time without a tenant can quickly outweigh a higher monthly rent. Aim for a realistic rent that balances demand, occupancy and long-term value.
