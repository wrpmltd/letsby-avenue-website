Letting questions landlords ask us.

How much rent could my property achieve?

It depends on the location, size, condition and who the property suits. A free rental valuation gives you a realistic figure based on comparable homes nearby. Understanding the Leeds rental market

What do I need before a tenant moves in?

Landlords need the right safety certificates and documents in place, including a gas safety record where there are gas appliances, an electrical installation condition report and an energy performance certificate, plus working smoke and carbon monoxide alarms. We'll check what you have and what's missing. Preparing your property to let

Which areas do you let in?

We're based in Stanningley and let properties across Leeds, Pudsey, Bramley, Farsley, Armley, Horsforth and the surrounding West Yorkshire area.

Can I switch to full management later?

Yes. Many landlords start with tenant find and move to full management later. Just let us know and we'll explain how the change works.
