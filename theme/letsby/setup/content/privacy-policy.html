Who we are

Letsby Avenue (North) Ltd trades as Letsby Avenue. For questions about information you share with us, email [la_email], call [la_phone] or write to [la_address].

This notice covers visits to this website and enquiries sent through it. If you become a landlord, tenant or applicant with us, we'll give you further privacy information that covers tenancy, referencing and management.

Information you provide

Our enquiry form asks for your name, email address, phone number, property address or postcode and a message. We also receive the information you choose to send by email or discuss by phone. Please only include what's relevant to your enquiry; don't send identity documents or bank details through the website.

Why information is used

We use enquiry information to reply to you, understand the property or service you're asking about and arrange the next step. Where you ask us to prepare a service for you, we rely on taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. For general correspondence we rely on our legitimate interest in answering enquiries and running the agency.

Sending an enquiry does not add you to a marketing mailing list.

Who may receive it

Your information is handled by our staff and by the providers who support the website and our business: our website is hosted in the UK by Krystal Hosting Ltd, and our email is provided by Google Workspace. We may also share information with professional advisers, or with authorities where we are legally required to. We don't sell your information.

How long it is kept

We keep enquiry information for as long as we need it to deal with your enquiry and any service that follows, to meet legal requirements and to handle any complaint or dispute. Enquiries that don't lead to a service are reviewed regularly and deleted when they're no longer needed. We don't keep information indefinitely just because we can.

Your information rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct mistakes, delete it, restrict how we use it or give you a portable copy. Some rights have conditions or exceptions. Contact us using the details above to make a request.

Your right to object

You can object to the use of your information for direct marketing at any time. You can also object to processing based on legitimate interests for reasons relating to your situation. Tell us which use you object to so we can consider your request.

Website analytics and cookies

With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how people use this website so we can improve it. Analytics is off by default: we don't load the Google tag until you choose to allow it. We turn off Google signals and advertising personalisation, and we don't use marketing tracking.

Google receives usage information such as the pages you visit, your device and browser, online identifiers and network information, and may process it outside the UK. See Google's privacy policy for details.

You can change your mind at any time using Cookie settings in the footer. If you turn analytics off, we stop tracking and remove our analytics cookies. This doesn't delete information Google has already received. Our cookie policy has more detail.

Questions and complaints

Please contact us if anything about our use of your information concerns you. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. You don't have to use our complaints process before contacting the ICO.
