03

Start with a conversation. Then make your move.

You don't need to have everything worked out before speaking to us. Bring your current management agreement and tell us what's happening with the property. If a contract term or charge is disputed, get independent advice before relying on a particular exit date.

  • Your notice terms. Check the minimum term, notice period and how notice must be served.
  • Your current agent's charges. Check any exit fees, continuing commission or other payments that may remain due.
  • The proposed handover date. Agree when responsibility changes and who handles rent and repairs in the meantime.

A proper handover. Nothing left to guesswork.

With your authority, we'll request the relevant information from your current agent, review what's received and follow up on missing items.

Tenancy & tenant communication

The tenancy agreement, relevant tenant and guarantor details, notices and important correspondence. We confirm how tenants can reach us.

Rent & account position

The rent schedule, payment history, arrears, landlord statements and any funds or invoices still to be accounted for.

Deposit & protection records

The deposit amount, scheme details and protection documents. We coordinate the appropriate scheme process and any required authorisations or updated information.

Safety, keys & ongoing work

Certificates, licences, inventories, inspection reports, keys and open maintenance. We identify upcoming dates and work that still needs attention.

Let's make the next step clearer.

Can I switch agent while my property is tenanted?

Often, yes. Changing the managing agent does not itself end the tenancy. The practical timing depends on your agency agreement and the arrangements for the handover. We'll discuss these with you before agreeing a start date.

What do I need to do with my current agent?

You'll need to authorise the change and comply with your agreement's notice requirements. Once instructed and authorised, we can liaise with the agent about the practical handover and keep you informed about anything that needs your input.

What will it cost to switch?

We'll explain our fees and terms before you commit. Your current agent may have notice-period charges, an exit fee or continuing commission under their agreement. Check these separately so you can assess the full cost of moving.

How will my tenants be told?

We'll plan the introduction and explain the new contact details and any payment changes from the agreed date. The handover should make clear who receives each payment and who deals with maintenance, so tenants aren't left with conflicting instructions.

What happens to the deposit?

We first establish where it is held, how it is protected and who is responsible for it. Any transfer or update follows the relevant scheme's process, with the required permissions and information. It isn't treated as an ordinary payment between agents.

Can I just talk it through first?

Absolutely. Tell us what isn't working and what you want from your next agent. We'll talk through the service and the practical steps, so you can decide whether Letsby Avenue is right for you.
